Our Story

Rooted in Craft,
Driven by Passion

Brewhaus was born in a small canal-side warehouse in the Jordaan neighbourhood. What started as an obsession with perfect espresso has grown into Amsterdam's most beloved specialty roastery — without ever compromising on quality.

🌍
Direct Trade Sourcing
We build long-term partnerships with farmers in Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, and beyond — paying well above Fair Trade prices.
🔥
Small-Batch Roasting
Our Probat roaster handles batches of just 15 kg, giving us precise control over every roast profile for maximum flavour clarity.
📦
Fresh to Your Door
We ship within 48 hours of roasting across the Netherlands and Europe, with compostable packaging only.

From Seed to Cup

How We Roast

Every batch follows a meticulously crafted roast curve — developed over months of cupping and refinement.

01
Green Coffee Intake
We receive fresh-crop green beans and cup them against our benchmarks before accepting any lot.
02
Profile Development
Our head roaster designs a unique temperature curve tailored to each bean's density and moisture content.
03
Small-Batch Roasting
Roasted on a Probat P15, each 15 kg batch is logged digitally and monitored throughout first and second crack.
04
Rest & Dispatch
Beans rest for 48 hours to degas, then are sealed in valve bags and shipped within 24 hours of packing.
